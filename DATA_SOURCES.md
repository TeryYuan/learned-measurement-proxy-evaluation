# Sources and access

The [22-file decision table](sources/REPLAY_INPUT_DECISIONS.csv), [excluded-field decisions](sources/REPLAY_EXCLUDED_FIELDS.csv), [projection column record](sources/replay_projection_columns.json) and [replay guide](DATA_ACCESS_AND_REPLAY.md) describe exactly what is proposed for release. Detailed original crosswalks and file-by-file review evidence are retained by the author outside this package.

|---|---|---|
| FDA DILIrank workbook, preserved 2026-10-07; separate V1 and V2 sheets | N/C/bridge labels; [official dataset](https://www.fda.gov/science-research/liver-toxicity-knowledge-base-ltkb/drug-induced-liver-injury-rank-dilirank-20-dataset) | [FDA policy](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/about-website/website-policies) checked 2026-10-23; FDA content generally public domain unless otherwise noted. The optional acquisition script and its [exact source manifest](sources/liver_source_downloads.json) download only the three specified original workbooks. A successful source download is not full training reproduction. Author-generated scores, arrays and aggregates are identified separately from upstream source data. No original paper, third-party reader program or private model cache has been added.
