Predicting Liver-Related In Vitro Endpoints with Machine Learning to Support Early Detection of Drug-Induced Liver Injury. Supplementary datasets SI002 and SI004 (2025-03-11), DOI https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrestox.4c00453.s002 and https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrestox.4c00453.s004. ## FDA and NHANES

DILI summaries retain provenance to FDA DILIrank 1.0/2.0 and the author's previously defined drug-level concern mapping; this revision changes no labels. Source: https://www.fda.gov/science-research/liver-toxicity-knowledge-base-ltkb/drug-induced-liver-injury-rank-dilirank-20-dataset. NHANES aggregate results derive from the study's existing 2003–2010 public-use survey and 2019 public-use linked mortality analysis. No participant records, raw cohorts, fitted models or prediction arrays are included.

The original source artwork is credited to its creators, not the manuscript author. The publication patch excludes manuscripts, prompts, private logs, dependency caches and baseline figures. No remote release, DOI or publication claim is made.
